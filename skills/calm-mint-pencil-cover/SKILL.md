# Calm Mint Pencil Cover

Use this skill to keep a stable article-cover visual language:

- ultra-wide 21:9 composition;
- hand-drawn colored pencil;
- low-saturation mint, sage, pale aqua, gray-green, off-white, and faint cool gray;
- peaceful, breathable, restorative mood;
- generous negative space;
- no text, logo, watermark, people, buildings, harsh contrast, saturated colors, or photorealism.

## Workflow

1. Use an image generation tool by default.
2. Preserve the fixed style constants.
3. Change only the requested scene or theme.
4. If the user gives only an article theme, choose a quiet natural scene that supports it.
5. Generate one polished cover first; iterate only if a core constraint is broken.

## Scene Mapping

- burnout or recovery: winding stream, quiet hills, mist, small grasses.
- uncertainty or transition: forest path opening into a pale clearing.
- rest or safety: still lakeside with reeds and soft morning fog.
- self-compassion: sheltered meadow, low wildflowers, soft sky.
- clarity or focus: minimal mountain ridge, open sky, thin path.
- flow or movement: slow river bend, stepping stones, drifting clouds.
- renewal or growth: seedlings, rolling fields, light after rain.
- loneliness or grief: foggy field or soft rain on leaves, never bleak or dramatic.

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a calm 21:9 article cover illustration in hand-drawn colored-pencil style.
Scene: {scene}.
Use a low-saturation mint green palette with soft sage, pale aqua, gray-green, warm off-white, and faint cool gray.
The mood should be peaceful, breathable, restorative, and introspective.
Include subtle paper grain, delicate pencil lines, soft diffused natural light, and generous negative space.
Keep the composition ultra-wide, uncluttered, balanced, and suitable for an essay cover.
No text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no buildings, no harsh contrast, no saturated colors, no photorealism.
```

## Quality Check

- Does it read as 21:9 cover art?
- Is the palette calm mint and sage rather than saturated green or blue?
- Does it feel quiet and breathable?
- Is there no text or watermark?
- Did the scene change while the visual language stayed consistent?
